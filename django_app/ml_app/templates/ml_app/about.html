About Mindello

Helping families notice early signs

Mindello is a warm, thoughtful screening companion that helps parents and healthcare professionals recognise early developmental patterns and feel more confident about next steps. It is designed to feel calm, clear, and respectful — never clinical or overwhelming.

Where it comes from

The screening draws on well-established developmental observations used in child development research, including social communication patterns, behavioural tendencies, and developmental history — combined with factors such as speech development, learning styles, and emotional wellbeing. It was built in collaboration with developmental research to ensure a thoughtful, informed approach.

How it works
  1. Share observations — Answer simple questions about your child's everyday behaviour.
  2. Gentle review — Your responses are thoughtfully compared with known developmental patterns.
  3. Clear guidance — A straightforward summary helps frame what to do next.
  4. Suggested next steps — Kind, practical recommendations on when to seek further support.
Built with care

Mindello uses a carefully designed approach to provide a guidance summary that supports — never replaces — your healthcare provider's judgment. Every result is reviewed through the lens of established developmental research.

Who it's for

Parents and caregivers who want a calm, structured first step. Clinicians looking for a quick, consistent summary to support — not replace — their professional judgment.

Important: Mindello supports early awareness only. It does not replace a professional medical diagnosis. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for diagnosis and care planning.

About Mindello

Early autism screening support

Mindello is an AI-assisted screening tool that helps parents and healthcare professionals notice early behavioral indicators and decide whether further evaluation may be helpful. It is designed to feel calm, clear, and respectful of the clinical process.

Clinical foundation

Screening draws on established behavioral measures used in autism research, including the AQ-10 Autism Spectrum Quotient, Q-CHAT-10, and Childhood Autism Rating Scale (CARS), alongside developmental history and co-occurring conditions such as speech delay, learning differences, and anxiety. Data used to build the tool comes from peer-reviewed research at the University of Arkansas Computer Science Department.

How screening works
  1. Behavioral questionnaire — Parents or clinicians answer guided questions about the child.
  2. Pattern analysis — Responses are compared with clinically studied indicators.
  3. Risk level estimation — A screening confidence score helps frame next steps.
  4. Recommendation output — Plain-language guidance on whether further evaluation is advisable.
Research-backed evaluation

We use multiple validated AI models to improve reliability and reduce false results. Models are continuously evaluated for clinical relevance. The system provides a screening confidence score — not a diagnosis.

Who it is for

Parents and caregivers seeking a structured first step. Clinicians who want a quick, consistent screening summary to support — not replace — their judgment.

Important: This tool supports awareness and early screening only. It does not replace professional medical diagnosis. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for diagnosis and care planning.
